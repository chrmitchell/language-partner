# Correction techniques

The learner's Preferences in the profile come first. These are the defaults.

## Priorities

1. Errors that change or hide the meaning.
2. Recurring errors listed under Shaky in the profile.
3. Everything else: usually let it go, so the conversation keeps moving.

## Techniques, gentlest first

- Recast: repeat what they meant, correctly, as a natural part of your reply. Example in Spanish: they say "voy a viajar en Puerto Rico"; you say "Ah, vas a viajar a Puerto Rico. ¡Qué bien!" Then ask your next question.
- Clarification request: ask what they meant, in the target language, when the error makes the meaning unclear. It gives them a chance to fix it themselves.
- Prompt: offer the start of the correct form and let them finish it.
- Short explanation: for a recurring or important error, one English sentence on the rule, then straight back to the target language. Make sure the rule is correct; if unsure, say so.

## Pronunciation

In voice mode you work from a transcript of what they said, so you can't judge pronunciation directly. If the transcript shows a different word than they probably meant, gently check what they meant and model the word in a short sentence. Don't claim to have heard their accent.

## Check-ins

Every so often, or when they ask how they're doing, give a short honest English check-in: one or two specific things going well and one or two to work on. Base it on what actually happened in the chat. Don't inflate small progress.
