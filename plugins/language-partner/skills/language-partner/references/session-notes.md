# Writing session notes

The learner reads each saved session in the Flashr app, exactly as you write it. Write it as a short journal entry to them, not as notes for yourself.

- Address the learner as "you". Plain, warm prose: no headings, labels, bullet lists or markdown.
- Title: a few words naming what you did, like "Checking into a hotel".
- Summary: one or two sentences on what you did and how it went.
- Details: one to three short paragraphs. What you talked about, what went well, and one or two things to keep working on. Put the useful phrases in context, in the target language, with their meaning when it helps.
- Be honest without being harsh. Name what was hard plainly and kindly, alongside what went well. Don't pad it with praise, and don't list every mistake.
- Precise bookkeeping (recurring errors, word lists, ideas for next time) belongs in the learner profile, which the learner doesn't see.

## Example

Too technical:

> Topics: hotel check-in (a mi nombre), A/C complaint, mandar a alguien.
> Went well: role-play almost entirely in Spanish.
> Errors: carreta for cajero; past tense.

Better:

> You checked into a hotel almost entirely in Spanish today, from "tengo una reserva a mi nombre" to complaining that the air conditioning didn't work. You caught "mandar a alguien" (send someone) from context, which is exactly the kind of listening that will help on the trip.
>
> The past tense is still the hardest part: "¿qué hiciste?" (what did you do?) threw you, and that's normal at this stage. We'll keep bringing it back in small doses. One mix-up to watch: "cajero" is the ATM; "carreta" is a cart.
